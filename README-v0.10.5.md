# Storm Chaser v0.10.5 — Ridgeline Cinema

The third supplied route film introduces Ridgeline Jumps, level six, at checkpoint 05. It shows the orange storm truck launching over a hill and landing in mud. The six-second film keeps its 898 × 512 frame proportions, speed and original sound, with short picture and audio fades. It is converted to Theora/Vorbis for Godot playback without upscaling.

The chase timer, score and hazards stay frozen while the film plays. It finishes naturally into the level, or can be skipped with Keep Chasing, Space, Escape or controller A. If video playback is disabled or fails, the existing Godot road preview with Mateo remains available. The supplied film itself has no visible Mateo; he remains in the gameplay truck.

Reach checkpoint 05 to unlock the replay in Mateo's Footage > Next > Checkpoint 05. The gallery now contains six dodge films and five checkpoint films. Unlocks survive restarting, and an existing valid save at checkpoint 05 or later also qualifies. Gallery playback preserves the saved chase and score.

The v0.10.4 square ninety-degree shortcut, controllable drift, lighter brown mud, wet ruts and lens splashes remain. All eight levels, original truck artwork, gameplay Mateo, earlier movies, audio and saves are retained. No Higgsfield or ElevenLabs credits were spent.

The release preview is recorded from the exported Linux game. It opens with the prerecorded supplied film playing in the checkpoint UI, followed by actual level-six driving, jumps and landings, then the earned gallery entry. Earlier stages are simulated under ordinary game rules. Offline capture is not a hardware-performance measurement.

Windows: extract the full ZIP and open Storm-Chaser.exe. Linux: extract and run ./Storm-Chaser.x86_64. Godot: import project.godot. Web: serve the complete folder over HTTP(S). See README.md for controls and campaign details.
