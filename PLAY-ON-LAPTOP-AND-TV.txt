STORM CHASER v0.17.0 — MATEO'S GARAGE

START ON WINDOWS
1. Extract the complete Storm-Chaser-Windows.zip.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click Storm-Chaser.exe.
3. Choose START CHASING, or RESUME CHECKPOINT if you have a saved run.

PLAY ON YOUR TV
Connect the laptop to the TV using HDMI (or a compatible video adapter).
On Windows, press Win+P and choose the TV display mode you prefer.
Press F11 in the game for fullscreen. Enable the TV's Game Mode if available.
The laptop runs the game; the TV displays it and can play its sound.

CONTROLLER
Connect a Windows-compatible controller by USB or pair it with Windows.
Left stick or D-pad: steer. A: boost. B: brake. X: transmit a probe.
Start: pause/resume. Use the D-pad and A to select menu options.
A/B/X: skip the ending. Start pauses the ending without skipping it.
Keyboard and mouse remain available.

KEYBOARD
A/D or arrows: steer. W/Up/Shift: boost. S/Down: brake.
Space: transmit a charged probe. P/Escape: pause. M: mute. F11: fullscreen.
Space/Enter: skip the ending. R on results: retry checkpoint or chase again.

THE MISSION
The truck drives forward automatically. Steer around debris, brake before
curves, recover from puddles and land straight after big jumps.
Send at least THREE probes when the probe indicator is ready.
Complete eight levels and the full tornado lap to reach the ending film.
Collect field data for your score and local top-five scoreboard.

Seven checkpoints save level entrances after an upgrade and cinematic.
A retry restores that checkpoint's hull, probes, upgrades and score.
Failed-attempt points are not added again. Winning clears the checkpoint.
The ending earns 2,000 data once; a complete survey also earns hull x 20.

MATEO'S FOOTAGE
Open the gallery from the base, pause menu or results. Select NEXT for
later checkpoints and THE FINALE. Completing the lap earns the final film.
Watching replays never changes your score or checkpoint.

MATEO'S GARAGE
Choose MATEO'S GARAGE at the base. Pick paint accents, wheels, roof
equipment, bumper armor and suspension trim; these change the look only.
CHASE SETUP is separate: STOCK keeps the original handling, while RALLY,
ARMORED and TURBO each trade one strength for a weakness.
Keyboard: Up/Down select, Left/Right change, Q/E or mouse drag to orbit,
R restores the stock look, Enter starts, Escape returns to base.
Controller: D-pad or left stick select and change, LB/RB orbit,
Y stock look, A or Start starts, B returns to base.
Your loadout is saved and shown in Mateo's Footage. A non-stock setup
is labeled on the scoreboard.

LAPTOP SETTINGS
DRIVING & DISPLAY offers LIGHTER GRAPHICS, CALM EFFECTS, steering recovery
and extra reaction time. Start with LIGHTER GRAPHICS if motion is uneven.
Assisted runs can earn every film and are marked on the scoreboard.

YOUR SAVES
Keep the existing Windows user account to retain saved progress.
Extract new versions into a fresh folder; the game uses the same save
location for records, checkpoints and settings.

This package is the playable game. The cinematic scenes are prerecorded
movies triggered by gameplay. Physical Galaxy Book2, TV and controller
performance still requires a test on your setup. Mobile is a separate build.
